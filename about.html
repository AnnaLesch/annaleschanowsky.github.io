I am a senior scientist at Fraunhofer IIS and a doctoral candidate at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, affiliated with AudioLabs Erlangen. With a background in audio and speech processing, I now work on whether conversational AI systems deserve the trust people place in them, across technical, human and governance dimensions. My dissertation examines how privacy and fairness interact in speech technologies.
