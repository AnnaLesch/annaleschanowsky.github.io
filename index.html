What I do

I build the evaluation methods that make AI governance enforceable. Legal frameworks like the EU AI Act and GDPR require voice and conversational AI systems to be transparent, privacy-preserving and fair but they don't specify how to test whether a system meets those requirements. I take a legal or ethical construct, operationalize it as something measurable, and run the perceptual or statistical evaluation that produces actual evidence.

In practice this means designing listening tests and user studies, building evaluation and audit tools, and translating EU AI Act / GDPR requirements into technical specifications for engineering teams.
