What I do

I build the evaluation methods that make AI governance enforceable. Legal frameworks like the EU AI Act and GDPR require voice and conversational AI systems to be transparent, non-manipulative, and non-discriminatory but they don't specify how to test whether a system meets those requirements. That's the gap I work in: taking a legal or ethical construct, operationalizing it as something measurable, and running the perceptual or statistical evaluation that produces actual evidence.

In practice this means designing listening tests and user studies, building evaluation and audit tooling, and translating EU AI Act / GDPR requirements into technical specifications for engineering teams.
