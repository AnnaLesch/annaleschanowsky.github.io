I build the evaluation methods that make AI governance enforceable — translating what the law requires of voice and conversational AI (informational self-determination, non-manipulation, non-discriminatory performance) into evidence that can actually be measured against real people, not just checked off a compliance list. Senior Scientist, Fraunhofer IIS / AudioLabs Erlangen; doctoral candidate, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg.

Two projects anchor this in practice: TransparentNLP, on the legal-construct side, and AI governance advisory for public/sovereign foundation models, on the institutional side. Publications, talks, teaching, and art & practice-based work each have their own page — linked below and in the navigation above.
