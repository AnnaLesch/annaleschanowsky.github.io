What I do

I build the evaluation methods that make AI governance enforceable. Legal frameworks like the EU AI Act and GDPR require voice and conversational AI systems to be transparent, privacy-preserving and fair but they don't specify how to test whether a system meets those requirements. I take a legal or ethical construct, operationalize it as something measurable, and run the evaluation that produces actual evidence.
