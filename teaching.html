Seminars

Data Governance Applications

Summer Term 2025 · Summer Term 2026 · FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg

Builds on Data Governance Foundations, extending into the practical application of data governance for AI systems — frameworks for data security, privacy, and legal/ethical compliance. Covers data quality and classification, privacy-enhancing technologies, and bias analysis in speech, image, and NLP data. Hands-on work includes creating data cards, using Python and Jupyter Notebooks to assess data quality, data cleaning, implementing privacy-enhancing techniques, and analyzing bias in voice data. Culminates in a group project designing a full data governance framework for a chosen AI application.

Data Governance Foundations

Winter Term 2024/2025 (with Birgit Popp) · Winter Term 2025/2026 · FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg

Introduces the foundations of data governance: legal requirements, ethical considerations, metadata management, and implementation. Starts from ethical and legal foundations and works through current AI topics such as training large language models and handling voice recordings. Includes analysis of real-world governance failures and successes, design choices under different international laws, ethical-dilemma role-play, and metadata management exercises. Culminates in a group project building a data governance plan for a hypothetical organization.
