I translate regulatory requirements, such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act, into measurable technical criteria for speech and conversational AI. This page lists the public consultations, calls for evidence, standards and industry guidelines I have contributed to, most of them as part of my work at Fraunhofer IIS. Related research is on the publications page, and the Conversational Privacy Patterns turn transparency requirements into design guidance.

Consultations & calls for evidence

Targeted consultation on copyright and AI

European Commission · consultation 29 Sep – 3 Nov 2026 · consultation page

Role: contributing author and technical lead of the response, with Fraunhofer IIS. The consultation covers the use of copyright-protected content in AI and copyright in scientific research, as part of the review of the Copyright in the Digital Single Market Directive (EU) 2019/790.

EDPB Guidelines 03/2026 on web scraping in the context of generative AI

European Data Protection Board · consultation 8 Jul – 30 Oct 2026 · consultation page · guidelines (pdf)

Role: led the response, with Fraunhofer IIS. The guidelines clarify how the GDPR applies to scraping personal data from public websites to train generative AI.

Call for evidence on the review of the Copyright in the Digital Single Market Directive and a possible new copyright initiative

European Commission · call for evidence, May 2026 · announcement

Role: contributed to the response, with Fraunhofer IIS.

Cybersecurity Labeling for Internet of Things (PS Docket No. 23-239)

US Federal Communications Commission · 2023 · FCC 24-26

Role: contributed to the comments submitted by the ISCA Special Interest Group on Security and Privacy in Speech Communication (SPSC), on privacy and security labels for IoT devices.

Standardization & industry guidelines

ITU-T P.807 (2016) Corrigendum 1: correction to the scoring formula in clause 6.4

ITU-T · July 2026 · ITU publication

Role: contributor, with Fraunhofer IIS. P.807 is the subjective test methodology for assessing speech intelligibility. The related open-source tool is SITool.

Data Security Specific to Voice (executive whitepaper)

Open Voice Network, Linux Foundation · V2.0 draft, 30 June 2022 · pdf

Role: contributor, acknowledged in the paper, which was developed by the Privacy and Security Work Group of the Open Voice Network's Technical Committee.
